About

Purpose, people and doing the work.

I believe businesses can do well and do good at the same time, and I have built my career on proving it.

As founder and CEO of Kin&Co, I led a multi-award-winning culture, communications and consultancy business that helped some of the world’s most influential brands place ethics and purpose alongside profit.

I care deeply about how people feel at work. Mental health, wellbeing and diversity have always come first in the teams I build, and every piece of work is designed to leave a long-term social and environmental legacy.
